# CRM acceleration phase 1: independent Shopify full-history export

## Scope and safety

- `api/crm-orders-bulk.js` is an independent, protected Vercel cron endpoint; it **does not alter CRM customer records or grades**.
- Requires the Vercel Shopify synchronization application's `read_orders`, `read_all_orders`, and `read_products` scopes. This is **not** the Feishu bot's token.
- Starts an asynchronous Shopify Bulk Operation, polls by its ID and archives the completed JSONL response into a private Vercel Blob. Operation state and raw export use versioned keys separate from existing 180-day snapshot and reconciliation.
- The Bulk operation is designed to export every available historical order (no 180-day date filter), line-item sale-time prices and current product metadata. Deleted products may have null metadata; this must not remove the historical order line.
- The raw archive is **not yet a validated financial ledger**. `totalRefundedSet` is only one raw aggregate; do not infer item-level net revenue by subtracting it from a merchandise subtotal. The next phase must reconcile refunds/transactions, currencies, cancellation, test orders and customer IDs before updating final M/F/R or LTV.
- `api/crm-orders-bulk-audit.js` separately scans the private JSONL export, deduplicates order IDs, checks child-parent linkage, excludes invalid orders, and flags discrepancies between successful refund transactions and Shopify's refunded total. This report is intentionally audit-only and may be incomplete for multi-currency/custom refunds; it never changes Feishu CRM customer fields.
- The Vercel cron is every 20 minutes; completed jobs are idempotently reported and not relaunched. Failed/cancelled jobs require investigation and do not silently restart.

## Gmail efficiency

The Gmail import now caches the customer email-to-record-count index for 30 minutes in a private Blob. Only valid customer rows are indexed when their quality field is populated. The communication records continue to be created with the existing thread-level duplicate checks; no sends or changes to customer master records occur.

## Verification after deployment

1. Check `/api/crm-shopify-health` and ensure all required scopes, including `read_products`, are reported; the exporter returns `paused` when absent.
2. Inspect the Vercel cron response: `started` → `running` → `archived` with a nonzero root order count. Do not expose signed export URLs.
3. Verify the versioned private archive includes order rows plus JSONL child rows linked using `__parentId`, and compare order counts to Shopify analytics. Check whether the export is large enough to require a separate durable downloader; 85-second download timeout is fail-and-retry, not data loss.
4. Test the seven customer IDs in the merchant's Shopify 180-day >$3,000 segment against the raw order ledger before trusting any CRM grades.
5. Do not run final grade reconciliation until the all-history ledger and refund semantics are validated.

See Shopify docs: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/bulk-operations/queries

## Seven-customer high-value financial sample (Draft, audit only)

- Set server-only \`CRM_FINANCE_SAMPLE_CUSTOMER_IDS\` to seven unique Shopify customer GIDs separated by commas. These must come from the merchant-approved seven high-value accounts, **not** guessed from 180-day M or generated by the dashboard. Never expose this setting in frontend code, issue comments, logs or static assets.
- The CRON_SECRET-protected \`/api/crm-orders-bulk-audit\` reads the private JSONL once, records each selected customer's order IDs, order dates, inclusion/exclusion reasons, refund evidence, historical merchandise net and rolling 180-day amounts, and archives results in a **private Blob**. No CRM tier or Feishu write occurs.
- `sampleAudit.status` is BLOCKED when seven Shopify sample IDs, per-customer source order counts, original order IDs, refund reconciliation or settled payment/currency evidence are incomplete, or when duplicates / over 100 evidence rows occur. `BLOCKED_SAMPLE_REFUND_RECONCILIATION` and `BLOCKED_SAMPLE_FINANCIAL_EVIDENCE` prevent financially inconsistent samples from appearing manual-ready. Otherwise the sample stays `PENDING_MANUAL_SHOPIFY_FINANCIAL_RECONCILIATION`. `sampleAudit.verified` remains false throughout.
- A reviewer must cross-check each of the seven against original Shopify order totals, currency, refunds, returns, order status and fulfilled product lines. The source JSONL may itself be incomplete due to the Shopify Bulk API, so a full-export count comparison and permissions review are also mandatory.
- Transaction refund totals and \`totalRefundedSet\` may differ for legitimate shipping/tax, currency and gateway adjustments. Mismatch is an audit signal, **not** proof of fraud or a basis for automatic customer regrading.
- If the sample is blocked, leave production RFM and customer grade unchanged. This PR has no deployment approval.
- A count-complete JSONL archive is **blocked** when any settled positive-merchandise order has zero associated child line items, even when root/total Shopify object counts match. This prevents accepting a partial product graph as complete evidence. The seven-account sample separately reports `BLOCKED_SAMPLE_MISSING_LINE_ITEMS`; no grade or RFM write is authorized.
